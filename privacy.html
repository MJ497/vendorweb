Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Vendor_M (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a vendor marketplace platform that allows vendors to upload, manage, and promote their products, while users can browse and order them. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app and related services.

2. Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide

  • Account Information: name, email address, phone number, and password.
  • Vendor Information: store name, product listings, descriptions, pricing, and uploads.
  • Subscription & Payment Details: limited data for vendor subscriptions (processed via Paystack or Google Play Billing).
  • Messages & Reviews: when you communicate or leave feedback within the app.

b. Automatically Collected Information

  • Device details (model, OS, app version)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Usage data for analytics and security

3. How We Use Your Information

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Information may be shared only with trusted service providers or as required by law.

5. Data Retention

Data is kept as long as necessary to provide services. Users can request account deletion at any time.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable security measures (such as encryption and authentication) to protect data, though no system is completely secure.

7. Your Rights

9. Children’s Privacy

Vendor_M is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.
